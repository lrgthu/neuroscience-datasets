# Neural Latents Benchmark '21 (`NLB-21` / `DANDI:000127–000130`)

## 1. Overview & Experimental Paradigm
- **Core Contribution**: Unifies evaluation of neural population latent-variable models (`LFADS`, `GPFA`, `NDT`, `SSM/Mamba`, `Switching LDS`) across four functionally distinct cortical regimes (`Pei et al., NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks, 2021`):
  1. **`MC_Maze` (`DANDI:000128`)**: `M1 + PMd` (`182` units, `2,295` trials, `108` curved/straight barrier reach conditions) — autonomous rotational motor dynamics.
  2. **`MC_RTT` (`DANDI:000129`)**: `M1` (`130` units) — continuous self-paced random-target reaching without delay periods (continuous control inputs).
  3. **`Area2_Bump` (`DANDI:000127`)**: Primary somatosensory `Area 2` (`65` units) — unexpected mechanical arm perturbations (sensory input transients).
  4. **`DMFC_RSG` (`DANDI:000130`)**: Dorsomedial frontal cortex (`182` units) — cognitive Ready-Set-Go time-interval reproduction without overt movement during timing.

## 2. Recording Specifications & Preprocessing
- **Format**: Standardized `1 ms` spike-count + continuous hand kinematics/force/eye-position **NWB** files loadable in 3 lines via `nlb_tools`.

## 3. Canonical Splits, Noise Ceiling & Modeling Pitfalls
- **Co-Smoothing Metric (`co-bps`)**: Measures out-of-sample Poisson log-likelihood (in bits/spike relative to a constant mean-firing-rate baseline) on **held-out neurons** given simultaneous **held-in neurons** on unseen test trials, paired with **forward prediction (`fp-bps`)**, **hand-velocity ridge decoding ($R^2$)**, and **PSTH $R^2$**.
- **Critical Modeling Pitfalls**:
  1. **Strict Positivity for Poisson Likelihood**: Clamp predicted firing rates $\hat{\lambda}_{n,t} \ge 10^{-9}$ (`softplus` or `exp` output head) so a single unexpected test spike never causes $-\infty$ `co-bps`.
  2. **Test Input-Driven Generalization**: Always evaluate across *both* `MC_Maze` (autonomous dynamics) and `Area2_Bump` / `MC_RTT` (external sensory/motor drive) to avoid overfitting to stereotyped ballistic trajectories.
