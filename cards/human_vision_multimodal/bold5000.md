# BOLD5000 Release 2.0 (`BOLD5000`)

## 1. Overview & Experimental Paradigm
- **Core Contribution**: Bridges classic scene/object neuroimaging and three canonical computer vision benchmarks (**SUN**: `1,000` scene images; **MS-COCO**: `2,000` multi-object interaction images; **ImageNet**: `1,916` object-centric images) using an unconfounded **slow event-related** design (`Chang et al., Scientific Data, 2019`).
- **Stimulus Design & Timing**: Each image is displayed for `1.0 s` followed by `9.0 s` of fixation (`10.0 s` per trial) while subjects perform a valence rating (`like / neutral / dislike`) to maintain engagement.
- **Subject Cohort**: `4` subjects (`CSI1–CSI4`); `CSI1–CSI3` completed all `15` functional sessions (`5,254` trials), whereas `CSI4` completed `9` sessions (`~3,108` trials).

## 2. Recording Specifications & Preprocessing
- **Acquisition Hardware & Resolution**: `3T` Siemens Verio, `2.0 mm` isotropic multiband EPI (`TR = 2.0 s`), accompanied by scene/object localizers defining bilateral `EarlyVis`, `LOC`, `OPA`, `PPA`, and `RSC`.
- **Release 2.0 Upgrade**: Reprocessed with **fMRIPrep** and **GLMsingle** (`Prince et al., eLife, 2022`), dramatically improving voxel reliability and encoding $R^2$ over the original v1.0 release.

## 3. Canonical Splits, Noise Ceiling & Modeling Pitfalls
- **Standard Evaluation Split**: Train on the `~4,803` unique single-presentation images; evaluate on the `113` repeated test images (`4` repeats in `CSI1–CSI3`).
- **Critical Modeling Pitfalls**:
  1. **Never Mix v1.0 and v2.0 Numbers**: Literature benchmarks prior to 2022 often report v1.0 betas, which have substantially lower noise ceilings—always specify `BOLD5000 v2.0 (GLMsingle)` in protocol headers.
  2. **Dataset-Source Confounds**: ImageNet images are object-centered while SUN images are panoramic indoor/outdoor scenes; stratify evaluation across `SUN / COCO / ImageNet` subsets to verify models are not merely classifying dataset origin.
